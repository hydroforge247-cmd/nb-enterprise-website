Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

NB Enterprise ("we", "us") respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

What we collect

This website does not ask you to fill in any forms. When you call, email or WhatsApp us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, number and what you need, so that we can quote, sell, deliver or repair.

Why we collect it

To answer your enquiry, process your order or repair, deliver it, and keep our records. We do not sell your details.

Who sees it

Our staff. We do not share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it or it is needed to deliver your order. If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that conversation.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The map on the contact section is provided by Google Maps. Staff sign-in keeps a temporary session in the browser so staff stay logged in while they work.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at info@nbe.co.za or on 018 294 4382. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).
